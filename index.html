Install

  1. Open the DMG and drag Elevique Flow into Applications.
  2. Open it. macOS says it can't verify the developer, because this free build isn't notarized. Click Done.
  3. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. You only do this once.
  4. Follow the Get started checklist: add a free key from console.groq.com/keys, allow the microphone and Accessibility, and set the 🌐 key to "Do Nothing".

Private by design

Audio and text go only to Groq, with your own key. History, notes and recordings stay on your Mac. The app's log never contains what you said.
